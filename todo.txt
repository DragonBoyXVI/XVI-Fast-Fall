
## Primary game loop
--- Pregame Steps
-- Player chooses a game mode
-- Player chooses a starting character

--- Game Loop
-- Player enters a shop, they can buy health/upgrades, as well as view their loadout
-- Player delves into a level, where they fight for their life until an abstract quota is met
-- Player lands and is awarded their spoils once quota is met
-- Difficulty is increased
-- Player given option to save and quit or continue
-- Restart loop

--- FailState Steps
-- Player dies
-- Their score final score is calculated and shown to them
-- Given option to save score to a local leaderboard
-- If they do, show the leaderboard, and highlight their score.
-- Return to main menu to start a new game


## Player Abilities
Move in all directions
Dash in any direction, being thrust in that direction and immune to damage while dashing
Shoot at threats below
Has a set amount of health
health carries between stages
can obtain powerups to increase health/damage (persist between levels)
special actions with the special button
